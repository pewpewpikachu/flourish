Application

Prep, press,
flourish.

From bare nail to bloom in under fifteen minutes — no dry time, no salon visit.

01

Prep the bed

Push back cuticles, buff the nail lightly, and wipe with the included prep pad so the bond has a clean surface to hold.

02

Press & hold

Match each tip to its finger, apply a thin layer of flourish glue, and press firmly for twenty seconds along the cuticle line first.

03

Let it flourish

Wait sixty seconds before using your hands. Wear for up to fourteen days, then soak off gently with the reapplication guide.
